<h2>📖 Overview</h2>

<p>kanto-gear is a dual-screen companion mod designed specifically for Gen1Recomp on Android handheld devices. This application enhances your Pokémon experience by adding a second screen interface, making it easier to navigate menus, check stats, and manage your team without interrupting gameplay.</p>

<p>Whether you are playing on a foldable phone, a tablet, or a handheld gaming device with a secondary display, kanto-gear provides a seamless way to keep important information visible at all times.</p>

<h2>❓ Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>Does kanto-gear work on any Android device?</h3>
<p>kanto-gear is designed for devices with a secondary display, such as foldable phones, tablets with HDMI output, or handheld gaming devices like the Logitech G Cloud. It may also work on standard phones in split-screen mode.</p>

<h3>Do I need to have Gen1Recomp installed?</h3>
<p>Yes, kanto-gear is a companion mod and requires Gen1Recomp to be installed and running on your device. It will not function as a standalone app.</p>
